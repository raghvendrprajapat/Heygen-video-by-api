If you have ever shipped an app, you know this feeling.

The code is done. The build is signed. And then you hit the store listing. Screenshots.

And suddenly you are not a developer any more. You are a graphic designer.

You open Figma. You hunt for a device frame. You write a headline, hate it, and write it again.

You try to line up five slides so they tell some kind of story.

Then you find out the App Store wants one set of sizes and Google Play wants another. iPhone. iPad. Android phone. Seven inch tablet. Ten inch tablet. Chromebook.

So you export the whole thing again. And again.

A full day gone. Sometimes two. And the screenshots still look like screenshots.

Here is why that costs more than most developers think.

Your screenshots are the last thing a visitor sees before they decide.

They judge your listing in about three seconds. They do not read your description. They swipe the images.

Raw simulator screens lose that tap. A designed set earns it.
