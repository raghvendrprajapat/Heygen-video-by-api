Create a 60-second vertical UGC-style product demo for ConvertScreen, a tool
that turns raw app screenshots into conversion-focused App Store and Google
Play screenshot sets.

This must read as a developer sharing a fix for a problem they hit — NOT as a
sponsored ad. No brand intro, no logo card, no "sponsored by", no discount code,
no "link in bio", no subscribe ask. The presenter is a peer, not a spokesperson.
Never say the words "sponsored", "partner", or "ad".

STYLE
Single presenter, mid-twenties to mid-thirties, plain dark solid top, neutral
dark background, soft key light. Handheld-at-arm's-length framing, filmed like
a selfie video, NOT a studio set. Rigid upright posture, no swaying, no hair or
face touching. Direct eye contact with the lens throughout.

Energy arc: hard and clinical on the hook, calm and instructional through the
steps, firm on the close. Machine-gun pacing, zero filler words, hard cut at
the end with no smile and no fade.

DELIVERY DIRECTION
- Hook: zero blinking, intense direct stare, staccato pace. Sharp downward
  right-hand chop on "screenshots".
- "THREE seconds" and "TEN store sizes": eyebrows raise on the number, heavy
  vocal stress.
- Steps one/two/three: slow the pace down, count them on fingers.
- "Pretty wins compliments. Structure wins installs.": precision pinch gesture,
  falling pitch.
- Close: lean slightly toward the lens, furrowed brows, stern. Hard cut.

SCREEN RECORDING
The attached video is the real product. Cut to it as B-roll under the step
section and the payoff — never behind the hook or the final line, which stay on
the presenter's face. Match the cuts to the narration:
- "Drop in your raw app screens" -> the upload and project-create screens.
- "writes the headlines and reorders your screens" -> the AI assistant panel
  generating copy, and the slides labelled Hook / Feature / Proof / CTA.
- "Export one ZIP. Every store size" -> the publishing-target dialog and the
  final review grid showing 20 exported images across four device sizes.
Zoom in on the screen recording so UI text is legible on a phone. Do not show
any error banners or toast messages that appear in the recording.

CAPTIONS
Burn in captions, bottom-centre, bold sans-serif, high contrast, a few words at
a time. Emphasise the numbers.

Follow the script below exactly, scene by scene. Do not add lines, do not
rewrite lines, and do not append a call to action.

SCRIPT

Your app isn't losing installs because of the app. It's the screenshots.

Most developers upload raw simulator screens. Nobody taps those.

A visitor judges your listing in THREE seconds.

The old fix? A designer. A week of waiting. A few hundred dollars. Then you redo all of it for TEN store sizes.

Here is how it works.

Step one. Drop in your raw app screens. Nothing to install.

Step two. The AI writes the headlines and reorders your screens into an actual conversion story. Hook. Feature. Proof. Close. Every slide gets a job.

Step three. Export one ZIP. Every store size, named and ready to upload.

And here is the part most people don't realize. Most screenshot tools stop at pretty. Pretty wins compliments. Structure wins installs.

Your screenshots are not decoration. They are your conversion rate.

So here is the real takeaway. Before you ship another feature, go look at your store listing. Fix that first.
